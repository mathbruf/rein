## Phase 6 — Model correctness & hit-rate (weakness audit 2026-08-03)

Findings from a full read of `model/score.py` + `docs/validation_report.md`. The model currently ranks the held-out reports **at chance** (AUC 0.497, p≈0.51); these are the concrete reasons why, ordered by expected impact. Each is a **structural/physics** fix (or a measurement/display fix), NOT a weight re-tune against the 37 reports — so every one must be validated **out-of-sample under the CV harness (IDEA 010)** before it is trusted. Tier-1 items are the likeliest causes of the at-chance result.

| 012 | 2026-08-03 | proposed (Tier 1) | **Wind *direction* → aspect-aware exposure** | The scorer uses only wind *speed*; `WeatherDay` carries no direction and neither weather source even fetches it (Open-Meteo asks `temperature_2m,wind_speed_10m,precipitation`; MET reads only `wind_speed`). "Shelter/leeward" is reduced to direction-agnostic TPI, so a hollow is "sheltered" and a ridge "exposed" regardless of where the wind comes from — but shelter is fundamentally slope **aspect relative to the wind vector**. Both APIs provide direction for free (`wind_direction_10m` / `wind_from_direction`). Plumb it through, derive per-cell aspect from the DTM, and make the exposure/lee term a function of aspect·wind. The brief calls wind direction a primary input; the model discards it. | Claude |
| 013 | 2026-08-03 | proposed (Tier 1, bug) | **Decouple the two regimes so they *switch*, not co-fire** | The insect driver is read at the field's warm/calm tail and the shelter driver at its cold/windy tail (`score.py:193–198`) — but every day has both a warm tail and a cold tail, so `p_ins` and `p_shl` are **both partially on at once**. Their cell targets are opposite (high+exposed vs low+leeward), so in the weighted sum they partly cancel → a flatter, near-chance surface. Worse: `calm_lowland` = 10th-pctl of the *exposure-amplified* wind, which the downscaling drives to ≈0 in every hollow, so the insect "calm" gate is **trivially true on every day** and synoptic wind barely gates the bug regime. Make the regime a genuine day-level switch (or a single continuous blend that can't self-cancel), and gate insects on the *area* wind, not a guaranteed-calm hollow. | Claude |
| 014 | 2026-08-03 | proposed (Tier 1) | **Inversion-aware temperature downscaling** | `downscale_weather()` applies a fixed 6.5 °C/km lapse rate (`score.py:156`), i.e. it always makes the tops colder. But calm, clear autumn mornings — exactly the hunt-window regime the downscaling was built to rescue — produce **valley inversions**: cold air pools low, valleys are *colder* than the slopes. The model has the temperature gradient's sign wrong in its highest-stakes case. Detect the inversion regime (calm + clear/low-precip + cool) and flatten or invert the lapse rate for it; validate. | Claude |
| 015 | 2026-08-03 | proposed (bug) | **Remove TPI double-counting** | TPI enters the score through two correlated channels: wind is *derived from* TPI (`score.py:157–158`) and then feeds `p_shl_cell`/`p_ins_cell`, AND `refuge`/`shelter` add `tpi_n`/`1−tpi_n` **again** directly (`score.py:202–203`). Terrain shape is effectively weighted twice, inflating the static-terrain signal relative to the actual weather. Route exposure through one channel only. | Claude |
| 016 | 2026-08-03 | proposed | **Rebalance the near-constant forage offset** | `W_FORAGE=0.4` is added flat, but Lordalen is ~89% open-alpine at forage≈0.85, so forage contributes ~+0.34 to *almost every cell* — a near-constant offset that compresses dynamic range and dilutes the weather signal (on low-signal days the map collapses toward static elevation + a constant). Either make forage relative (subtract the field mean) or gate it so it discriminates rather than offsets; pairs with IDEA 007 (vegetasjonsdekke) to give it real within-alpine variation. | Claude |
| 017 | 2026-08-03 | proposed | **Rank/percentile transform for the displayed surface** | The 0–1 "probability" is a min-max stretch (`score.py:223–224`): one outlier cell rescales the whole map and the displayed transparency wash is outlier-sensitive. Ranking metrics (AUC) are unaffected, but the *image* and any absolute reading are distorted. Use a percentile/rank transform for display so the colour wash reflects relative standing, not distance to a lone extremum. | Claude |
| 018 | 2026-08-03 | proposed | **Validate the assumption thresholds** | The regime ramps `INSECT_T_LO/HI`, `COLD_T_*`, `WET_MM_FULL` (`score.py:76–84`) are marked "ASSUMPTION (hunter unsure)" yet the whole regime switching hinges on them.
